Good to know

Still wondering? Message reception on WhatsApp, Zalo, Line, KakaoTalk or Messenger — or call +84 901 825 789.

Why is Herbal Spa Da Nang so popular?

A premium, clean and peaceful space; highly skilled, experienced therapists; and a wide range of treatments — from relaxation to specialised therapies.

What extras do guests receive?

Every session ends with a complimentary light refreshment — hot soup, mango dessert, fruit, coconut cake or herbal tea, varying by season. Traveller-exclusive promotions run regularly.

Are there private rooms for couples or families?

Yes. Let us know when you reserve and we'll arrange the most suitable room.

Can I shower before or after my treatment?

Absolutely — clean shower facilities are available. Just ask reception.

Are personal lockers available?

Yes. Every guest gets a personal locker for the whole visit.

Do you accept credit cards or international payments?

Yes — Visa, MasterCard and other international and contactless methods. Contact us ahead if you plan to use a specific option.
